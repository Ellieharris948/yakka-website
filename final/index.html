Here to help

A few good questions.

How does Yakka protect my payment?

Your customer’s payment is held in a dedicated, ring-fenced account until the job is confirmed complete. A clear job breakdown records what both sides have agreed.

When does the tradie get paid?

Payment is released within 24 hours of confirmation, or automatically after 7 days if the customer doesn’t respond, subject to any open dispute.

What if something isn’t right?

Raise a dispute with supporting photos and evidence. Only the disputed part of the job stays held while it is reviewed. Decisions are made within 5 working days.

Can I pay for materials or in stages?

Yes. Tradies can request a partial payment for materials before work starts, or arrange milestone payments for longer jobs. Both parties agree to the payment request in the app.

Are there monthly fees?

There’s no monthly subscription. A 2% customer protection fee and a 5% tradie fee apply. Review the payment breakdown in the app before agreeing to a job.
